OneShot

What is OneShot?

OneShot is a top-down adventure puzzle RPG where you play as Yourself guiding a young Cat-person named Niko! in the game, you help niko return home after waking up in a strange house and finding the sun! Your main objective is to help niko return the sun into The Tower; an ancient, tall building that used to light the whole world before the sun went missing. It isone my favpurite games as an indie-game lover and developer.

My favourite Oneshot character(and why i like them) :>

My personal favourite character is Alula! in the game, you interact with Alula's brother, calamus, to learn that his little sister has gone missing. After finding Alula, you find out that she is ,simply put, CUTE! she is one of the characters that make you feel like you are a parent who is willing to do anything in thier power to protect thier child, in this case being Alula! her cheerful persona and curious nature would definitely make anyone fall like her character!
